Privacy Policy: MASK SHIFT

Last updated: 4 October 2026

MASK SHIFT ("the app") is developed by Löwentech GmbH. The app itself works without collecting personal data. This policy explains what that means, and where personal data does reach us: when you contact us, and when you test a beta version through TestFlight.

1. Controller

Löwentech GmbH

Urbanstraße 84, 10967 Berlin, Germany

Email: info@lowentech.dev

Managing director: Özer Arslan

2. The short version

  • No account or login.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics or advertising SDKs, no game server.
  • The app does not send your game data to us.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • If you email us or take part in a TestFlight beta, we receive the data described in sections 6 and 7.

3. Data stored on your device

The app stores your progress (solved levels, best results) and your settings (haptics, color symbols) locally on your iPhone. This data never leaves your device through the app and we cannot access it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

4. In-app purchase

You can buy the full game with a one-time in-app purchase. The purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment, billing and your purchase history under its own privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. The app only asks Apple whether the full game was purchased on your Apple account, so it can unlock it and restore it on a new device.

5. Information Apple may share with us (App Store version)

If you have agreed in your iPhone settings to share analytics or crash data with app developers, Apple may provide us with aggregated usage statistics and crash reports through App Store Connect. These do not identify you personally. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

6. Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to answer your request.

  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your request concerns the app you use, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in answering inquiries).
  • Retention: two years after your request has been resolved, unless statutory retention obligations require longer.
  • Recipients: our email hosting provider Hostinger, which processes the messages on our behalf under its data processing agreement.

7. Beta testing with TestFlight

If you test a pre-release version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares test information with us, regardless of the analytics setting in section 5. This can include:

  • your name and email address, depending on how you were invited;
  • device model, operating system version, app version and build;
  • usage information (installs, sessions) and crash reports;
  • feedback and screenshots you choose to send through TestFlight.

We receive this data from Apple, not from the app. We use it only to test and improve the app before release.

  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in testing the app before release with testers who chose to take part).
  • Retention: until six months after the public release of the tested version; if that version is never released, until six months after the beta test ends. The data is then deleted.
  • Apple's TestFlight privacy notice: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/

You can stop testing at any time by uninstalling the beta or leaving the test in the TestFlight app, and ask us to delete your test data at info@lowentech.dev.

8. Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for example the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit). For the app itself we usually hold no data about you. For any question, contact us at info@lowentech.dev.

10. Our website

This policy covers the app. Our website has its own privacy policy: https://lowentech.dev/privacy

11. Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example a new feature that needs data, we will update this policy before releasing that version.
